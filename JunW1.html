Meta Ad Policy -- Safe But Strong
Mandatory
Do NOT use
  • Named hotel brands (Four Seasons, Ritz, Westin, Marriott, Hyatt) -- use "5-star hotels" generically
  • Named competitor pillow or bedding brands
  • Personal attributes -- "your neck pain," "your snoring," "you are exhausted"
  • Medical claims -- "cures," "ends," "fixes," "stops" a condition
  • Person before/after that implies a health or body transformation
  • Professional-authority claims (doctors, psychologists, chiropractors recommend) unless substantiated
DO use
  • Generic category references -- "5-star hotels," "hotel-grade fill"
  • Observational, universal framing -- "the way mornings feel," not "your tiredness"
  • Product specs -- "down alternative," "hypoallergenic," "holds its loft"
  • Documented proof -- PureWow 95/100, Oprah Daily Sleep O-Wards 2024, 794,000 sleepers, 94% stat, 100-night trial
  • Price -- $69 is documented, safe to use
  • Product-only before/after (flat pillow vs lofted FluffCo)
Rule of thumb: if a headline calls out the viewer ("you are X"), it is unsafe. If it states a universal observation or a documented fact, it is safe.

5 to 10 seconds, no voiceover

Standing Instruction for Blitzads

Guiding principle for the whole batch: people buy explanations before products. Every asset should make the purchase feel obvious by explaining something, not just by showing a nice pillow.

Section discipline: Section 1 keeps the proven format skeletons and varies within them. Section 2 is one psychological lens per LP, each asset to its own LP URL. Section 3 is the test budget across 4 explanation concepts plus a small motion probe.

Naming is mandatory. Every asset follows Blitzads - Fluff - [Section] - [Angle] [MonthBatch] - [number]. This is how we read performance per section and per angle after launch. An asset that is not named correctly cannot be measured.

Meta safety is non-negotiable. No named hotel brands, no personal-attribute call-outs, no medical claims, no person before/after. Documented proof only (PureWow 95/100, Oprah Daily Sleep O-Wards 2024, 794,000 sleepers, 94% stat, 100-night trial, $69).

If two assets within the same concept could be mistaken for each other, they are not distinct enough. Regenerate.
