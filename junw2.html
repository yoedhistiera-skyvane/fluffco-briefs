Meta Ad Policy -- Safe But Strong
Mandatory
Do NOT use
  • Named hotel brands -- use "5-star hotels" generically
  • Named competitor pillow or bedding brands
  • Personal-attribute call-outs -- "your hot flashes," "your pregnancy insomnia," "you are in pain"
  • Medical claims -- "cures," "ends," "stops," "treats" any condition (critical for pregnancy and menopause audiences)
  • Pregnancy safety or health claims of any kind
  • Person before/after implying a health or body transformation
DO use
  • Observational, universal framing -- "the nights that run warm," not "your hot flashes"
  • Product specs -- "down alternative," "hypoallergenic," "breathable," "holds its loft"
  • Documented proof -- PureWow 95/100, Oprah Daily Sleep O-Wards 2024, 794,000 sleepers, 94% stat, 100-night trial
  • Price and the real promo -- $69, up to 66% off (substantiated)
  • Product-only before/after (flat pillow vs lofted FluffCo)
Extra caution this batch: the pregnancy and menopause audiences are health-adjacent. Keep all copy observational and product-anchored. Never claim the pillow helps a medical condition. If a headline could read as a health promise, rewrite it as an observation about comfort or temperature.

Standing Instruction for Blitzads

The goal of this batch is audience feedback. Section 2 is the priority. Each new audience runs in its own ad set, so every asset must be unmistakably made for its audience in the first second, not a generic pillow ad with a swapped line.

Naming is mandatory and now includes the audience token: Blitzads - Fluff - [Section] - [Audience] - [Angle] [MonthBatch] - [number]. This is how each asset maps to the right ad set and how we read which audience responded. An asset named wrong cannot be measured.

Health-adjacent audiences (Pregnancy, Menopause) stay observational. Never claim the pillow helps a medical condition, eases pregnancy, or treats hot flashes. Frame around comfort and temperature only. If a line could read as a health promise, rewrite it as an observation.

The discount lives in 10 assets only. Up to 66% off appears solely in Motion B. Everything else holds the standard $69 and award price story so the offer test reads cleanly.

Airbnb hosts are a different buyer. Frame around guest reviews, durability through turnover, and outfitting every room. Not personal comfort.

Meta safety holds throughout: no named hotels, no personal-attribute call-outs, no medical claims, no person before/after. Documented proof only. Numerals in headlines, no em dashes.
